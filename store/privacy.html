ClassSnag Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Extension version 1.1.0

ClassSnag monitors fitness-class availability on VirtuaGym websites and can request a booking when you enable Auto Book.

Information the extension uses

ClassSnag reads the class page you open, including its URL, class name, availability, capacity, booking buttons, reservation status and booking-error messages. It uses this information in your browser to monitor openings, request bookings and show status updates and notifications. It does not read passwords or session cookies, or browse unrelated websites.

Storage and sharing

Your Auto Refresh, refresh interval and Auto Book preferences are stored using Chrome's sync storage. Chrome may synchronize these settings through your Google account according to your browser configuration. Monitoring start times and refresh counters are stored locally. The monitored class URL appears in the monitor tab's address and may be retained by your browser's history or tab/session features. Class names may appear in local desktop notifications.

ClassSnag does not operate a backend and does not transmit your data to its developer. It contains no analytics or advertising services. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending.

Requests to your gym

Refreshing a class page and clicking its booking button send normal requests to your gym's VirtuaGym website using your existing signed-in session. That website receives and processes these requests under its own privacy policy. ClassSnag does not change the gym's reservation rules.

Embedded class monitor

The extension can display a class page inside a monitor tab. For these embedded class requests initiated by ClassSnag, it removes the site's frame-blocking and content-security-policy response headers to permit embedding. This rule is limited to HTTPS VirtuaGym class pages initiated by this extension.

Your controls and retention

Auto Refresh and Auto Book are off by default. Turn them off in the popup to stop future automatic actions; a request already sent to the gym may still complete. Close monitor tabs to stop their monitoring. Settings persist until changed or removed; local monitoring counters are cleared when monitoring stops or a reservation is recognized. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage, subject to Chrome's storage and synchronization behavior. Browser history and gym records are managed separately through Chrome and your gym.

Limited use

ClassSnag's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the monitoring and booking features described here.

Questions

For questions or support, visit the ClassSnag issue tracker. Issues are public; do not include passwords, membership details or other private information.
