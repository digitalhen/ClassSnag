Set it up once, then keep Chrome and your class tab open.

01

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Does it guarantee a spot?

No. Other members may book first, and your gym’s membership rules and booking windows still apply. ClassSnag keeps watching when an opening gets away.

Can I book two spots?

Use the separate accounts or family-booking options your gym supports. Two tabs signed in to the same account don’t create two member identities. Each monitored page stops when its reservation is recognized.

Does my browser need to stay open?

Yes. Keep Chrome and the class tab open, with your gym account signed in. Turn off Auto Refresh or close the tab when you want to stop monitoring.

Will it work with my gym?

ClassSnag is designed for VirtuaGym class pages. Gym layouts can vary. Use it where your gym permits automation, and check the setup guide for troubleshooting.

How do I install it?

ClassSnag has been submitted to the Chrome Web Store and is awaiting review. For now, download the free developer preview and follow the installation guide.
