Install the preview ↗

Chrome 116+ · Chrome Web Store release in preparation

  1. 01

    Choose your class

    Sign in to your gym’s VirtuaGym website and open the class you want to attend.

  2. 02

    Turn on monitoring

    Enable Auto Refresh and choose a 1–60 second interval. Enable Auto Book if you want ClassSnag to request a spot.

  3. 03

    Wait for confirmation

    If a spot opens, ClassSnag requests it. If someone gets there first, it keeps watching. A notification follows the site’s confirmation.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Your gym’s rules still apply.

A reservation isn’t guaranteed. Keep Chrome and the class tab open, and use automation only where your gym permits it. For two people, use the separate accounts or family-booking options your gym supports.

BUILT TO STAY LOCAL

Your class. Your browser.

No analytics, ads, or developer-operated backend. Settings can sync through Chrome; class-page and booking requests go to your gym as usual.
