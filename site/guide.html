GET STARTED

Install ClassSnag in Chrome.

ClassSnag runs in Chrome on your gym’s VirtuaGym class pages. You’ll need Chrome 116 or newer and an existing gym account.

ClassSnag has been submitted to the Chrome Web Store and is awaiting review. You can install the developer preview from the ZIP below. It is not yet a Chrome Web Store installation.

Download v1.1.0 ZIP ↓

Install the preview

  1. Download the ZIP and extract it into a folder you’ll keep on your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and enable Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted ClassSnag folder.
  4. Pin ClassSnag from Chrome’s Extensions menu for easy access.

Watch a class

  1. Sign in to your gym’s VirtuaGym website and open a class.
  2. Open ClassSnag from the toolbar. Enable Auto Refresh and choose a refresh interval. Small timing variations are normal.
  3. Enable Auto Book if you want the extension to request a reservation when an opening appears. Both settings are off by default.
  4. Keep Chrome and the class tab open. A successful click alone is not a confirmed booking: ClassSnag waits for the page to show your reservation.

Settings apply to your monitored classes and can sync through Chrome. To stop future automated actions, turn off Auto Refresh or close the class tab. A request already sent to your gym may still complete.

Common questions

What happens if someone gets the spot first?

ClassSnag dismisses the “Class is full” response and resumes watching. If the booking response never arrives, it refreshes to check the reservation state before deciding what to do next.

Can I reserve two spots?

Use the separate accounts or family-booking options provided by your gym. Two tabs in the same signed-in account do not create separate member identities or bypass your gym’s limits. Each monitored page stops when its reservation is recognized.

Does ClassSnag guarantee a booking?

No. Other members may reserve the opening first. Your membership, booking window, gym rules and the site’s availability still apply. Use automation only where your gym permits it.

Why isn’t it working on my gym’s page?

Check that you’re signed in, the URL is a VirtuaGym class page, and Auto Refresh is enabled. Gym layouts may differ. If you just updated the extension, reload it at chrome://extensions and refresh the monitored tabs. You can report a problem through the support link below.

What data does it use?

Class details and reservation status are processed in your browser. Settings use Chrome Sync. Your gym receives normal page loads and booking requests. Read the privacy policy for storage and embedded-monitor details.
